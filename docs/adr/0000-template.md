# ADR-NNNN: Short decision title

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, source evidence, and why a durable decision is needed. Do not include secrets, private prompts, or personal data.

## Decision

State one decision clearly. Include scope and any compatibility, migration, security, privacy, or platform consequences that affect implementation.

## Alternatives

List plausible alternatives considered, including the status quo where relevant, and why each was not chosen.

## Tradeoffs

Record benefits, costs, risks, and follow-up conditions. Separate verified facts from assumptions.

## Review Status

Not reviewed. Record review status separately from decision status. When reviewed, identify the reviewer or review role and date in both the metadata and this section.
